Startup considerations

Low barrier to entry
Action cams are low-cost relative to professional cameras. GoPro HERO13 retails ~$500–600 CAD. DJI Osmo Action 5 Pro ~$400–550 CAD. 3–5 units + accessories can launch a pilot.
Favorable
Business model options
1) Owner-operator: you own inventory, rent direct · 2) P2P/marketplace: list others' gear for 5–15% commission · 3) Hybrid. Start owner-operator, validate demand, expand to marketplace.
Decision point
Insurance & damage policy
Critical risk item. Require credit card hold (standard $200–500 deposit per unit). Consider optional damage waiver fee (+$5–15/day). Use a rental agreement with liability clauses.
Must address
Booking platform
Booqable or RentMy recommended for SMB rental ops · Handles inventory, bookings, payments, double-booking prevention · ~$50–100/month
Low cost tool
Physical presence vs. online + delivery
Pickup/drop-off model works without a storefront. Meetup at a fixed location or offer delivery. Reduces overhead significantly for a phase-1 operation.
Flexible
